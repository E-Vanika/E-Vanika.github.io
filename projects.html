01

RESPONSIBLE ML

ACTIVITY RECOMMENDATION / LIVE

Zippy Kiddo

OPEN PRODUCT ↗

A privacy-conscious recommender for children aged 3–12. It filters by age, time, space, energy, supervision, and available materials without collecting unnecessary personal data.

The intelligence boundary

Deterministic eligibility and safety rules remain authoritative. A compact TF‑IDF retrieval model improves ranking, while the service falls back safely to deterministic ordering if the artifact cannot load.

The model lifecycle

The reviewed catalogue is embedded into a Kaggle training kernel. Training must clear an 80% top‑1 retrieval gate. The validated model is published to Hugging Face, locked by revision and SHA‑256, then downloaded and verified during the application build.

02

GROUNDED AI

MULTI-BRAND SERVICE PLATFORM / LIVE

Monika Glam Up

OPEN PRODUCT ↗

A customer-facing bridal service platform with an AI concierge, enquiry workflow, reviews, administrator-managed content, and an MCP interface. One codebase also serves Crafts by Vani and Mamo Self Care without leaking brand identity.

Grounded, not improvised

The concierge retrieves from reviewed service and pricing knowledge. Deterministic fallbacks keep useful answers available without an external model provider, and business owners retain final control over availability and quotes.

Deployment as a product concern

GitHub Actions tests and builds distinct Vercel projects, then verifies each brand’s title, favicon, and health response. A collision fails delivery rather than silently publishing the wrong experience.

03

SITE RELIABILITY

PRODUCTION-LIKE LAB / LIVE

Single‑VM SRE Platform

OPEN PLATFORM ↗

A complete reliability system on a constrained OCI VM, designed to show the controls and trade-offs clearly rather than claim that a single node is highly available.

Safe delivery

Git-SHA images, serialized deployment, readiness gates, deterministic rollback, and an external smoke test ensure the exact tested revision is the one serving traffic.

Operational evidence

A realistic 99.0% SLO, error budget, burn-rate policy, RED/USE signals, cross-service OpenTelemetry traces, runbooks, failure drills, and a blameless postmortem make reliability inspectable.
